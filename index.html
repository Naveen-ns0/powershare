How this works

RollerCoin splits each coin's block reward among every player mining that coin, in proportion to power. Your share is your power ÷ league power, and your estimated reward is that share multiplied by the block reward.

A new block is generated roughly every 10 minutes by default, though this can shift slightly depending on activity — adjust the block-time field above if you've noticed a different pace in-game.

You'll find your power, your league's total power for a coin, and the current block reward directly inside RollerCoin's mining screen for that coin.

Worked example

Say your power is 8.596 Eh/s, your league's total power for DOGE is 9.400 Zh/s, and the block reward is 29.323 DOGE every 10 minutes.

Converted to the same unit, your share is 8.596 × 10¹⁸ ÷ 9.400 × 10²¹ ≈ 0.0914% of the league. Multiplied by the block reward, that's roughly 0.0268 DOGE per block — about 3.861 DOGE per day, and ≈115.84 DOGE per month at that same rate.

Glossary

Quick definitions if any of the terms above aren't familiar yet.

Power

Your mining power for a specific coin, shown in your RollerCoin mining screen — measured in hashes per second (h/s), usually written with a prefix like Gh/s, Th/s, Ph/s, Eh/s, or Zh/s depending on scale.

League power

The combined power of every player mining that same coin in your league — including your own power. It's the denominator in the share calculation.

Block reward

The total amount of a coin paid out and split among all miners each time a new block is generated, roughly every 10 minutes by default.

Share

Your power divided by league power — the fraction of each block reward you're estimated to receive.

Gh/s, Th/s, Ph/s, Eh/s, Zh/s, Yh/s

Units of hashing power, each 1,000× the one before it: Giga-, Tera-, Peta-, Exa-, Zetta-, and Yotta-hashes per second. As RollerCoin's total network power grows, larger units become more common in league totals.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official RollerCoin tool?

No — PowerShare is an independent, fan-made calculator and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by RollerCoin.

Does this pull live data from RollerCoin?

No. You enter your own numbers from the game, and the calculator estimates your reward from them. Nothing is fetched automatically, and nothing you enter is stored.

Why isn't my estimate matching what I actually earned?

League power changes constantly as other players join, leave, or reallocate power — so a reward calculated a few minutes ago can already be out of date. Treat this as an estimate for planning, not a guarantee.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. Nothing is saved — the moment you close or refresh the page, your inputs are gone.

Does adding power always increase my reward?

Almost always, but by less than you might expect — because adding power increases both your share's numerator and the league's total power (the denominator) at the same time. The What If I Add Power tab calculates this correctly and shows the real gain.

Can I use this for any coin — BTC, LTC, DOGE, ETH, SOL, POL?

Yes. The calculator doesn't care which coin you're mining — just enter that coin's league power and current block reward, and label it however you like.

How do I find my league power in RollerCoin?

Open the mining screen for the coin you want to check inside RollerCoin — your power and the league's total power for that coin are both shown there.

How accurate is the estimate?

As accurate as the numbers you enter, at the moment you enter them. Since league power shifts as other players join or reallocate, treat the result as a snapshot estimate rather than a guaranteed payout.

Is PowerShare Calculator free?

Yes — free, with no account, no sign-up, and no limit on how many times you can use it.

What's the best RollerCoin calculator to use?

Any RollerCoin calculator is only as good as the numbers you give it. PowerShare uses the same power-share formula RollerCoin itself describes, runs the math transparently, and adds a What If I Add Power comparison so you can see the real effect of buying more power before you spend anything.

Is there a free RollerCoin calculator online?

Yes — PowerShare Calculator is completely free, works in any browser, and doesn't require creating an account or linking your RollerCoin profile.
