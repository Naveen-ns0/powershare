How this works

RollerCoin splits each coin's block reward among every player mining that coin, in proportion to power. Your share is your power ÷ league power, and your estimated reward is that share multiplied by the block reward.

A new block is generated roughly every 10 minutes by default, though this can shift slightly depending on activity — adjust the block-time field above if you've noticed a different pace in-game.

You'll find your power, your league's total power for a coin, and the current block reward directly inside RollerCoin's mining screen for that coin.

Worked example

Say your power is 8.596 Eh/s, your league's total power for DOGE is 9.400 Zh/s, and the block reward is 29.323 DOGE every 10 minutes.

Converted to the same unit, your share is 8.596 × 10¹⁸ ÷ 9.400 × 10²¹ ≈ 0.0914% of the league. Multiplied by the block reward, that's roughly 0.0268 DOGE per block — about 3.861 DOGE per day, and ≈115.84 DOGE per month at that same rate.

Glossary

Quick definitions if any of the terms above aren't familiar yet.

Power

Your mining power for a specific coin, shown in your RollerCoin mining screen — measured in hashes per second (h/s), usually written with a prefix like Gh/s, Th/s, Ph/s, Eh/s, or Zh/s depending on scale.

League power

The combined power of every player mining that same coin in your league — including your own power. It's the denominator in the share calculation.

Block reward

The total amount of a coin paid out and split among all miners each time a new block is generated, roughly every 10 minutes by default.

Share

Your power divided by league power — the fraction of each block reward you're estimated to receive.

Gh/s, Th/s, Ph/s, Eh/s, Zh/s, Yh/s

Units of hashing power, each 1,000× the one before it: Giga-, Tera-, Peta-, Exa-, Zetta-, and Yotta-hashes per second. As RollerCoin's total network power grows, larger units become more common in league totals.

Permanent power

The mining power your account holds from placed miners, unaffected by day-to-day activity. This is the figure RollerCoin uses to place you in a league — separate from temporary power earned through games, which fluctuates and doesn't affect league placement.

Global Bonus Power

A combined percentage from every bonus-power miner you currently have placed and active. This single percentage multiplies your entire account's permanent raw power — not just the miner that provides it. Owning a bonus miner without placing it contributes nothing.

Rack Bonus

A percentage boost that applies only to miners placed in a specific rack, based on that rack's own bonus. Rack Bonus stacks additively on top of Global Bonus Power rather than multiplying with it.

How Miner Power Impact works

Buying a new miner — or swapping one out for a better one — always changes your permanent power, but by exactly how much isn't obvious at a glance. That's the gap the Miner Power Impact tab closes: enter your current numbers and the miner you're considering, and it tells you the precise power increase before you spend anything.

This matters most for players sitting near a league threshold. Cross it by even a small margin and you don't just move up — you land at the very bottom of the next league, often facing a much larger, more established pool of miners for the same block reward. Knowing the exact increase in advance lets you get as close to a threshold as you want, without accidentally tipping over it.

The calculation keeps two separate effects apart, since RollerCoin itself treats them differently: Global Bonus Power, a single combined percentage from every bonus miner you have actively placed, which multiplies your entire account's raw power — and Rack Bonus, a percentage that only affects miners sitting in one specific rack and stacks on top rather than multiplying in. Mixing these two up is the most common source of a wrong estimate, which is why the tool asks for them as separate fields.

It also deliberately leaves out one thing: a "new total power" figure. Total power depends on every other miner already racked on your account, which this tool doesn't have visibility into — so instead of guessing, it reports the increase only, which is the number that actually matters for league planning. It also only ever uses permanent power, never temporary power from games, since temporary power doesn't affect league placement.

Why use it

Compare a miner purchase or upgrade against your current power before buying — see if it's worth it, or if it pushes you further than intended. Plan a miner swap by entering the old miner's stats alongside the new one, so replacement scenarios are covered as easily as a fresh purchase. And get a straight answer to "how much does this actually add" without needing a spreadsheet or manually working through global-bonus and rack-bonus math by hand.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official RollerCoin tool?

No — PowerShare is an independent, fan-made calculator and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by RollerCoin.

Does this pull live data from RollerCoin?

No. You enter your own numbers from the game, and the calculator estimates your reward from them. Nothing is fetched automatically, and nothing you enter is stored.

Why isn't my estimate matching what I actually earned?

League power changes constantly as other players join, leave, or reallocate power — so a reward calculated a few minutes ago can already be out of date. Treat this as an estimate for planning, not a guarantee.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. Nothing is saved — the moment you close or refresh the page, your inputs are gone.

Does adding power always increase my reward?

Almost always, but by less than you might expect — because adding power increases both your share's numerator and the league's total power (the denominator) at the same time. The What If I Add Power tab calculates this correctly and shows the real gain.

Can I use this for any coin — BTC, LTC, DOGE, ETH, SOL, POL?

Yes. The calculator doesn't care which coin you're mining — just enter that coin's league power and current block reward, and label it however you like.

How do I find my league power in RollerCoin?

Open the mining screen for the coin you want to check inside RollerCoin — your power and the league's total power for that coin are both shown there.

How accurate is the estimate?

As accurate as the numbers you enter, at the moment you enter them. Since league power shifts as other players join or reallocate, treat the result as a snapshot estimate rather than a guaranteed payout.

Is PowerShare Calculator free?

Yes — free, with no account, no sign-up, and no limit on how many times you can use it.

What's the best RollerCoin calculator to use?

Any RollerCoin calculator is only as good as the numbers you give it. PowerShare uses the same power-share formula RollerCoin itself describes, runs the math transparently, and adds a What If I Add Power comparison so you can see the real effect of buying more power before you spend anything.

Is there a free RollerCoin calculator online?

Yes — PowerShare Calculator is completely free, works in any browser, and doesn't require creating an account or linking your RollerCoin profile.

How do I calculate how much power a new RollerCoin miner will add?

Use the Miner Power Impact tab: enter your current raw power and global bonus %, then the new miner's power and bonus %. If you're replacing an existing miner, add its stats too. The tool returns the exact permanent power increase, accounting for both global bonus and rack bonus separately.

What's the difference between Global Bonus Power and Rack Bonus in RollerCoin?

Global Bonus Power is one combined percentage from every bonus miner you have placed, and it multiplies your entire account's permanent power. Rack Bonus only affects miners in one specific rack and adds on top separately — RollerCoin's own blog confirms the two don't multiply together.

Will buying a new miner push me into a higher RollerCoin league?

It can, if the power increase crosses your league's threshold. The Miner Power Impact tab shows you the exact increase before you buy, so you can see whether a purchase keeps you under a threshold or pushes you past it.

How do I avoid accidentally crossing a RollerCoin league threshold?

Check the exact power increase a miner purchase or swap would add before you buy it, using the Miner Power Impact tab. That lets you get as close to a threshold as you want without tipping over into a league where you'd start at the bottom against much stronger competition.

Does temporary power from RollerCoin games count toward my league?

No — league placement is based on permanent power from placed miners only. Temporary power earned through games fluctuates constantly and doesn't affect which league you're in, which is why this calculator never asks for it.

Can this calculator handle a miner swap, not just a new purchase?

Yes. The Miner Power Impact tab has fields for an old miner's power and bonus % (both default to 0 for a fresh purchase). Fill them in for a swap and the tool nets out the old miner's contribution before calculating your increase.

Where do I find my permanent power and global bonus % in RollerCoin?

Both are shown on your account's profile/statistics page inside RollerCoin. Only actively placed bonus miners count toward your global bonus % — one sitting unplaced in your inventory contributes nothing.
